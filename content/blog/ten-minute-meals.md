Most weeknight cooking fails for the same reason: the gap between deciding to cook and actually eating is too wide. You have to wash, chop, season, cook, and then clean. whatever cooker collapses that gap by doing all of it in one sealed cycle.

## What actually happens in ten minutes

The appliance runs three stacked layers. The first washes raw vegetables and meat. The second chops, slices, and stirs. The third steams and cooks. Each layer cleans itself after use, so nothing cross-contaminates between steps.

You drop ingredients in, choose a method, and walk away. Ten minutes later the meal is done and the machine is already rinsing itself.

> **TL;DR** — whatever cooker takes raw ingredients in and returns a fully cooked meal in ten minutes, with no washing, prepping, or cleanup required from you.

## Cooking methods compared

Different dishes need different heat. Here is how the built-in methods line up.

| Method | Best for | Typical time | Cleanup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steam | Vegetables, fish, dumplings | 8–10 min | Self-rinse |
| Stir-fry | Noodles, mixed veg, tofu | 9–10 min | Self-rinse |
| Slow-cook | Stews, braises, beans | 45–90 min | Self-rinse |
| Rice | Grains, porridge | 20–30 min | Self-rinse |
| Noodles | Ramen, pasta, soups | 10–12 min | Self-rinse |

## What you still control

Seasoning, portion size, and ingredient choice stay entirely in your hands. The companion app tracks protein, fiber, vitamins, and minerals, and can plan meals around what you already have.

1. Pick a base — rice, noodles, or vegetables.
2. Add a protein — chicken, tofu, fish, or beans.
3. Choose a method and press start.
4. Eat when it beeps.

## Common questions

### Do I need to chop anything first?

No. Whole vegetables and cuts of meat go in as-is. The middle layer handles chopping and slicing.

### Does it really clean itself?

Yes. Each layer auto-washes after the cycle, so there is no scrubbing and no cross-contamination between steps.

### Can I cook more than one portion?

Yes. The capacity supports multiple servings, and the app scales ingredient amounts accordingly.
