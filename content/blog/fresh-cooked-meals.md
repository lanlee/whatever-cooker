## TL;DR

Fresh cooked means cooked recently and chilled, never frozen or shelf-stable. You can get one on a weeknight five ways: cook from raw yourself, use a meal kit, order prepared delivery, buy from a grocery counter, or let whatever cooker cook raw ingredients for you in 10 minutes.

## What "fresh cooked" actually means

Search this phrase and you land on product pages, each selling one answer; almost none define the term. The definition we use: a fresh cooked meal is fully cooked, then chilled — eaten within days, not weeks. Not frozen. Not shelf-stable. That line matters because freshness has a clock on it, and the clock is short.

## How long a cooked meal stays fresh

None of the ranking pages states the deadline. Per the [USDA](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety), cooked leftovers keep **3 to 4 days** in a fridge at 40°F or below, or **3 to 4 months** frozen; refrigerate within **2 hours** of cooking (1 hour above 90°F) and reheat to **165°F**. That's the fine print under every "fresh" claim above: a Monday delivery is at the edge by Friday.

## What weeknights actually allow

In the [BLS 2025 time-use data](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/atus.t01.htm), the average American spends **0.71 hours a day** — about 43 minutes — on food prep and cleanup. Women average **0.91 hours**; men, 0.51. Only **65 percent** do any at all on a given day. The five routes aren't competing over recipes. They're competing over who absorbs those 43 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a meal kit a fresh cooked meal?

No. A kit is raw, pre-portioned ingredients — you cook them. The result is fresh; the kit isn't.

### Are prepared deliveries actually fresh?

Factor states its meals are "fresh, never frozen," and CookUnity ships chef-cooked meals weekly. A "keep refrigerated" label with a short date means fresh cooked; "keep frozen" does not.

### How long does a fresh cooked meal keep?

Three to four days refrigerated (USDA), if chilled within two hours of cooking and reheated to 165°F.

### What's the fastest route on a weeknight?

A grocery-counter meal you reheat, or whatever cooker: raw ingredients in, meal out 10 minutes later.
